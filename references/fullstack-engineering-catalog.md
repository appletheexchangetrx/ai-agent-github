# Fullstack Engineering Catalog — 15 Domains

1. **Fullstack Architecture** — layer boundaries, modularity, dependency flow and ownership.
2. **System Analysis** — actors, requirements, constraints, business rules and acceptance.
3. **Frontend-Backend Integration** — client/server interaction, API use and failure behavior.
4. **API Contract Management** — schemas, versioning, validation, error shapes and drift.
5. **Data Flow Engineering** — source of truth, mapping, synchronization, caching and consistency.
6. **Authentication Integration** — identity/session propagation, refresh, logout and lifecycle.
7. **Authorization Integration** — UI/API/database permissions and tenant/resource isolation.
8. **Database Integration** — persistence, transactions, migrations, queries and integrity.
9. **Business Workflow** — state transitions, orchestration, idempotency and invariants.
10. **Security Integration** — trust boundaries, secrets, validation and injection risks.
11. **Performance Engineering** — client/server latency, network, queries, caching and resources.
12. **Integration Testing** — service/database boundaries and failure cases.
13. **E2E Testing** — critical journeys, permissions, browser and realistic test data.
14. **Deployment Engineering** — coordinated releases, environment config, migrations and rollback.
15. **Production Readiness** — observability, resilience, security, recovery and operational docs.

E-commerce when applicable: registration, discovery, variants, cart, checkout, payment, fulfillment, stock, refunds and customer account. Verify consistency and concurrency.
