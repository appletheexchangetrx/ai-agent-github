# Quality Assurance Catalog — 12 Domains

QA independently assesses evidence. Select checks by risk; do not run irrelevant suites.

1. **QA Strategy** — quality objectives, risk model, test levels, environment and exit criteria.
2. **Test Planning** — traceability, scope, cases, data, dependencies and prioritization.
3. **Unit Testing** — isolated logic, boundaries, deterministic behavior and assertions.
4. **Component Testing** — UI semantics, interactions, state and accessibility.
5. **Integration Testing** — service/database boundaries, failure handling and consistency.
6. **Contract Testing** — API schemas, compatibility, versioning and provider/consumer expectations.
7. **E2E Testing** — critical journeys, permissions and cross-layer workflows.
8. **Regression Testing** — affected areas, fixed defects, critical paths and compatibility.
9. **Visual & Accessibility Testing** — responsive layout, keyboard, focus, contrast and semantics.
10. **Security Testing** — authorization, input boundaries, secrets and abuse cases.
11. **Performance & Reliability Testing** — latency, resilience, timeout/retry and recovery in safe environments.
12. **Quality Gates & Reporting** — pass/fail/blocked, coverage, defects, flaky tests, residual risk and recommendation.

Design positive, negative, boundary, edge, permission, timeout and recovery cases as relevant. Never claim coverage or pass without evidence.
